Treat altitude as fluid emphasis, not fixed classification.

- **Horizon**: the broad idea, bigger picture, significance, or question that stays with you. feels visionary, but not eye-rolling slogan. answers so what?
- **Compass**: the working direction or frame that organizes the subject. gives shape, direction, mission.
- **Ground**: the concrete claims, evidence, decisions, actions, implementation, or details. feels grounded.

Treat language like "higher," "lower," "toward the Horizon," or "more Ground" as relative movement. Shift the response's center of gravity toward the requested altitude while connecting the other registers when useful. If no direction is named, move naturally among them. This is not a template nor a request for a 3-part response, just shared directional vocabulary.
